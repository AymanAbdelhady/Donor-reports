# Presenter guide: a 10-minute demo

The goal is to show an organisation that **a beautiful, personal donor report takes minutes, and that the tool is theirs from the first click.**

## Before the meeting (5 minutes)

1. Open the demo site and sign in with **Fill in for me**.
2. On the dashboard **Overview**, press **Reset demo** so you start clean.
3. Optional but powerful: have the organisation's **logo** (PNG) and **2–4 photos** of their work saved on your laptop.
4. Make one report in advance (Poster or Journey, 8 pages) to show if the connection is slow.

## The demo

### 1. "Here's the problem" (1 minute)
Thanking donors well takes hours: gathering facts, writing, designing, checking. So most donors get a generic receipt or nothing at all.

### 2. "Make it yours" (2 minutes): Dashboard → Branding
- Type **their organisation name** and press **Save branding**. A logo is drawn automatically in seconds.
- Click a **Quick look** close to their colours, or type their hex colours.
- **Upload their logo** and drop **their photos** into the **Photo library**.
- Under **CEO & letter**, type their CEO's name.

> Everything you just changed (the Studio, the illustrations, every report) now carries their brand.

### 3. "Thank a donor" (4 minutes): Open Studio
- Tap the **mic** and say one long sentence, e.g. *"The Naidoo family gave fifty thousand rand to our school feeding programme in Soweto. It fed 300 children every school day this year."*
- Answer one or two of the assistant's questions, or tap a suggestion.
- Show the **report card** filling in on the right.
- Choose a **style** and **8 pages**, then press **Create my report**.

### Optional: "And in Arabic" (2 minutes)
- Press **عربي** in the Studio header: the whole Studio turns Arabic and right-to-left.
- Start a new report, choose **Report language: العربية**, and create it. Every page is mirrored and written in Arabic.
- In the dashboard, open **Languages** to show the Arabic names, greeting, questions and writing style.

### 4. "The result" (2 minutes)
- Scroll through the report: the cover, the CEO letter, impact, story, spending and thank-you.
- **Edit wording** live (change the title), then **Download PDF**.
- Mention: style and length are locked after creation to keep AI costs predictable; wording and photos stay editable.

### 5. "Control and cost" (1 minute): Dashboard
- **Users:** each staff member gets their own sign-in.
- **Usage:** see who made which reports and the AI cost per person.
- **Assistant & questions:** change what the assistant asks, in their own words.

## Questions you may get

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is it our data? | Each organisation gets its own separate installation, users and storage. |
| What does it cost to run? | Hosting plus Claude usage. The dashboard shows the AI cost per report and per person. |
| Arabic? | Yes: the Studio and the reports, with Arabic writing style and wording set in the dashboard. |
| Faith-based wording? | Optional: add a blessing line in any language, their own giving types (e.g. Zakat, Tithe) and closing quotes. |
| Our own templates? | The nine styles follow their colours and logo; bespoke designs can be added. |
| No photos? | The library and brand-coloured illustrations fill gaps, and are labelled as illustrative. |
